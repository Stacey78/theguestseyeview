Privacy Policy

The Guest’s-Eye View handles business inquiries, restaurant identities, visit findings, and final reports as confidential information. This policy explains the website’s current information practices.

Information provided by you

You may choose to contact The Guest’s-Eye View by email at hello@theguestseyeview.com. When you do, the information you include—such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, city, location count, and audit questions—may be used to respond to your inquiry, scope a potential engagement, provide services, and maintain a business record of our conversation.

How the website contact experience works

The website’s current inquiry form opens your device’s email application with a pre-filled message. The form does not independently transmit its contents to this website’s server or store the form data in a website database. Once you send the email, your message is handled by the applicable email provider and The Guest’s-Eye View mailbox.

How information is used

Information is used to communicate about audits, proposals, scheduling, service delivery, and related business administration. The Guest’s-Eye View does not sell personal information or restaurant information. It does not publish client inquiries, restaurant identities, visit findings, or reports as public reviews.

Confidential service information

Actual client engagement materials are treated as confidential and shared only with the client and the client-authorized recipients, subject to the terms agreed for that engagement. Confidentiality does not prevent disclosures required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and safety.

Service providers and links

This website is hosted through third-party infrastructure and uses third-party asset delivery and email services. Those providers may process standard technical information, such as IP address and browser request data, to deliver the website or email service. This site may link to external websites; their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Security and retention

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No internet transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, service delivery, business records, or legal obligations.

Your choices and contact

You may ask to update or delete personal contact information by emailing hello@theguestseyeview.com. Questions about this policy may be sent to the same address.
